## What it is

Client-friendly AML/KYC/KYB + screening + compliance toolkit for SMEs that lack a compliance department.

## What ships today (product shape)

- Individual verification (KYC) and business verification (KYB / UBO chain)
- Capability links: `/v/{code}` client journey (login-free), `/r/{code}` / results for parties
- **Client pays** via Stripe; optional Connect **markup** on the **order** (not in the URL)
- Free compliance toolkit (program / risk / training style content — SEO wedge)
- Personr for ID verification; OTP login (4 digits, durable rate limits)

## What it is not (yet)

Enterprise case management, full transaction monitoring, reminting old KYCs from competitors.
